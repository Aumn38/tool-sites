What is Ideogram 4.5?

Ideogram 4.5, released on September 30, 2026, is Ideogram's most precise image editing model. Earlier models regenerate the whole picture on every edit, so faces shift, text warps and quality erodes round after round — a failure Ideogram calls drift. Version 4.5 is built to confine each change to exactly the region you specify and keep untouched elements stable across long edit chains, at native 2K resolution. Ideogram also announced distribution partnerships with Picsart, Runway, Pika and Leonardo AI, and pricing runs across four quality tiers reported from 0.8¢ to 22¢ per image.

That precision only pays off if your instructions are precise too. A vague prompt makes the model guess; a structured prompt — design type, subject, exact quoted text in reading order, hex colors, layout — leaves nothing to chance. That is what the builder above assembles for you.

How to write a good Ideogram 4.5 prompt

  • Lead with the design type. "A menu board…", "Product packaging…" — the first words set the model's whole layout logic.
  • Quote every word that must appear. Text in quotation marks is treated as literal copy. List lines in reading order and give each a role (headline, price, call-to-action) so size and placement come out right.
  • Use hex codes for brand colors. "Teal" is a guess; #0F766E is an instruction.
  • One change per edit turn. 4.5's strength is multi-turn editing — spend it wisely. Change the headline, check it, then change the background. Bundling five changes into one turn brings drift back.
  • Say what stays. In every edit, explicitly list what must remain unchanged: other text, faces, palette, layout.
  • Draft cheap, render once. Iterate at the lowest quality tier, then render only the final keeper at top quality.
  • Turn Magic Prompt off for exact text. It expands and rewrites prompts — great for vague ideas, risky for menus and packaging.

Frequently asked questions

What is Ideogram 4.5?

Ideogram's image model released September 30, 2026, focused on precise multi-turn editing: change one specified part of an image while the rest stays intact across consecutive edits, at native 2K resolution.

How much does Ideogram 4.5 cost?

Per-image pricing across four quality tiers, reported from 0.8¢ (economy) to 22¢ (top tier). Iterate drafts cheaply and render the final at top quality.

Should I turn Magic Prompt off?

Yes when exact text matters. Magic Prompt rewrites your prompt, which can alter quoted copy. For menus, posters and packaging, keep it off and use a fully written prompt.

What is Zoom Editing?

Editing a small region at the image's full original resolution — demonstrated on a 4,016 × 6,016 px source — then stitching it back seamlessly. Ideal for logos, labels and fabric detail.
