What ClipPrompt is

ClipPrompt is a video reverse-prompt tool: it analyses a short video and writes a shot-by-shot prompt in Wan 3.0 format that can reproduce a similar clip. Wan 3.0 is the video model format the tool supports; ClipPrompt is an independent tool and is not affiliated with the Wan model provider.

The preview: nothing leaves your browser

The current preview build runs entirely in your browser. Selecting a video file only previews it locally; no video, frame, audio or file is uploaded anywhere, and the preview makes no network calls at all. The preview requires no account, no sign-up and no login.

The live version: what is sent, and why

When the live reverse tool launches, submitting a video for analysis will send sampled frames and audio from that clip to the reverse engine provider (Alibaba DashScope, model Qwen3-VL) solely to produce your prompt. That data is used only for your request: we do not sell it, we do not use your videos to train models, and we do not keep copies of your videos after processing. Prompts produced from your clip are returned to you in the browser.

Payments and credits

Payments are handled by Creem, our merchant of record. We never receive, store or see your card details. Reverse credits are tied to the email address you pay with: the live tool unlocks them when you enter that email. We keep only the minimum record needed to honour your credits (payment email and remaining credit count).

Tracking, cookies, analytics

ClipPrompt sets no tracking cookies, runs no advertising, and uses no third-party analytics. The site is a static tool page plus the live reverse endpoint when it launches.

Your choices

You can use the preview without giving us anything. For the live version, you can ask us to delete your credit record by writing to peter.12312367@gmail.com from the email you paid with. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
