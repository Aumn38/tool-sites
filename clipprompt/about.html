About ClipPrompt

ClipPrompt turns a short video into a structured video-generation prompt with a shot table, in Wan 3.0 format.

What it does

Give ClipPrompt a clip and it breaks it down into subject, shots, camera and sound, then composes a copyable prompt that could have made a similar video. Analysis is done by Qwen3-VL. Wan 3.0 is the video model format the tool supports; ClipPrompt is an independent tool and is not affiliated with the Wan model provider.

Current status

The live reverse tool is being wired up. The current page is a preview build: it shows the full output format with a worked example ("Night Market Noodles", 12 seconds, 4-shot table) and runs entirely in your browser — no video leaves your device and no account is needed. The preview will be replaced by live analysis once wiring is complete.

Pricing

Your first reverse is free. After that, 10 reverses cost $5 one-time, taxes included. Payments are handled by Creem, our merchant of record, and reverse credits are tied to the email address you pay with.
